## Risks for the animation pass

- **S07–S09 aerials:** the drone moves, so the plates need tracking to the footage. Check with a short test first.
- **Presenter scenes:** the bar passes in front of the presenter. Nothing goes behind them without a matte.
- **Depth of field and motion blur:** not in this pass. Add per scene once moves are approved.
- **Music and sound design:** none yet; the reel's own audio plays.
