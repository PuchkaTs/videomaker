## The reference she sent

Facebook reel 1125360733644045 (downloaded to the scratchpad only): **RE/MAX WIN** ad for another agent, «Мэргэжлийн агент Баттөгс», 45.6 s, 1080×1920, 30 fps, -14.3 LUFS, one continuous motion-graphics piece (no hard cuts).

- **Concept:** «Би хүн биш»: a glowing AI voice orb introduces itself as Баттөгс's AI helper; «Листээс гүйлгээ хүртэл бид хоёр 17 ажил хийдэг».
- **Structure:** a counter «0/17 ажил» runs through the reel; each task appears as a UI card tagged **AI** or **БАТТӨГС** (price research, owner meeting, right price, photos, ad post, reel, page, groups, unegui.mn, remaxwin.mn, 24-hour replies, booking, weekly report, viewing, negotiation, contract, deal). Ends «ХУРД · ИТГЭЛ», then the agent's photo, name, red phone pill, «Мессенжерээр ч бичиж болно».
- **Look:** navy-to-red gradient, white/red bold sans type, word-by-word captions with one red accent word, comet-light transitions, RE/MAX logo always on top.
- **What works:** a strong counter that turns "agent work" into a visible amount of work; the agent's face and phone at the end; readable captions.
- **Weak points to beat:** the agent only appears in the last 8 s (the first 3 s show an orb, not a person); 17 small UI cards go by too fast to read; flat 2D cards; a generic stock palette; no real apartments on screen.

We don't copy its look, words or brand. RE/MAX branding stays out unless Гантуяа is a RE/MAX agent and sends the logo.
