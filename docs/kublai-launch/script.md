# KublAi launch video: script and storyboard

**Working title:** KublAi: анхны зурвасаас эцсийн хүргэлт хүртэл
**Format:** founder-led launch, three demos that get harder each time
**Length:** 2:06 (126 s) · 1920×1080 · 30 fps · 3,780 frames
**Language:** Mongolian voiceover with burned-in Mongolian subtitles. English lines are for reference only.
**Ending:** `public/kublai/brand-voice-ending.mp3` (4.28 s) plays over the closing wordmark.

The one claim the whole video proves: **KublAi doesn't just reply to messages. It runs the sale from the first message to the final delivery.** Each demo adds one new ability, and each one is harder than the last.

> Lines marked **[ ]** are placeholders. Fill them with real facts only: years, customer numbers. Have a native speaker proofread the Mongolian before recording.

## 1 · Founder and the claim · 0:00–0:16

Сайн байна уу, би Пүрэвдорж, KublAi-г үүсгэн байгуулагч. Бизнес эрхлэгчид өдөр бүр олон зуун зурвасад хариулж, захиалга, хүргэлтээ гараар хөтөлдөг. Бид [X] жил энэ асуудлыг шийдэхээр ажилласан. KublAi бол зүгээр нэг чатбот биш. Зурвасад хариулаад зогсохгүй, борлуулалтыг тань эхнээс нь дуустал гүйцэтгэдэг AI агент.

*(EN: Hi, I'm Purevdorj, founder of KublAi. Business owners answer hundreds of messages every day and track orders and deliveries by hand. We spent [X] years working on this problem. KublAi isn't just a chatbot. It doesn't stop at replying. It's an AI agent that runs your sale from start to finish.)*

## 2 · Demo 1: the first message · 0:16–0:42

**Picture:** split screen for this demo and the next two.
- **Left 40%:** the customer's phone (Instagram DM), in a device frame.
- **Right 60%:** the KublAi agent inbox: the conversation, the agent's steps, and the product it found.

**Sequence**
1. Title card slides in for 1 s: `01 · Анхны зурвас` (01 · First message).
2. Customer types: «Энэ цүнх хар өнгөтэй байгаа юу? Үнэ хэд вэ?» *(Is this bag available in black? How much?)*
3. Right side: the agent looks up the store catalog, and a highlighted "searching products" step appears.
4. The agent replies on the phone with a **product carousel**: photo, price, colors, an "Order" button.
5. A small timestamp badge: `Хариулсан: 3 сек` *(Replied in 3 s)*. **Only use a time that matches the real recording.**

**VO (MN):** Эхлээд энгийн жишээ. Үйлчлүүлэгч Инстаграмаар бичлээ. Агент таны барааны каталогоос шууд хайж, зураг, үнэ, өнгөтэй нь хариулна. Шөнө ч, амралтын өдөр ч.
*(EN: First, a simple one. A customer writes on Instagram. The agent searches your catalog and answers with photos, prices and colors. At night, on weekends.)*

**Sound:** soft UI clicks synced to the messages, piano continues.

**Motion:** the phone slides in from the left (ease-out, 18 frames), the inbox from the right. Gently zoom (100→108%) into the carousel when it appears.

**Assets:** screen recordings of a test Instagram account messaging a KublAi agent connected to a demo store with real product photos.

## 3 · Demo 2: the lead · 0:42–1:08

**Sequence**
1. Title card: `02 · Боломжит худалдан авагч` (02 · The lead).
2. The customer says they want it. The agent asks for a delivery address and phone number, and the customer answers.
3. Right side: a **CRM deal** card is created automatically: name, phone, product, pipeline stage.
4. The conversation's lifecycle tag animates from **New lead → Hot lead**.
5. **Control beat:** the owner clicks into the conversation and types a personal note («Танд бэлэг савлагаа үнэгүй!», *free gift wrapping for you!*). The agent steps aside. This shows the human is always in charge.

**VO (MN):** Одоо илүү ахисан түвшин. Агент хаяг, утасны дугаарыг асууж, CRM-д шинэ гэрээ автоматаар үүсгэнэ. Харилцагчийн төлөв шинээс халуун руу шилжинэ. Хүссэн үедээ та өөрөө яриаг үргэлжлүүлж болно.
*(EN: Now a step up. The agent asks for the address and phone number and creates a CRM deal automatically. The lead moves from new to hot. And whenever you want, you can step in yourself.)*

**Sound:** a subtle "whoosh" when the deal card appears, a soft chime on the lifecycle change.

**Motion:** the deal card flies from the chat into the CRM column (24 frames). The lifecycle tag morphs color.

**Assets:** recordings of the agent inbox, CRM deal view and lifecycle tag, plus a human reply sent from the inbox.

## 4 · Demo 3: the order and the delivery · 1:08–1:38

**Sequence**
1. Title card: `03 · Захиалгаас хүргэлт хүртэл` (03 · From order to delivery).
2. The agent creates the **order** and sends a **QPay invoice** in the chat. The customer pays on their phone (use test mode).
3. Right side: payment received. Lifecycle goes **Payment → Customer**, and the order status changes to **Confirmed**.
4. Cut to the **Kublai Delivery** app: a driver accepts the order, the status becomes **In transit**, a live map shows the route.
5. The customer's phone shows a delivery update, then **Delivered**. The customer sends a thank-you emoji.

**VO (MN):** Хамгийн хэцүү нь — захиалга. Агент захиалга үүсгэж, QPay нэхэмжлэх илгээнэ. Төлбөр орж ирмэгц захиалга баталгаажиж, хүргэлт замд гарна. Үйлчлүүлэгч бараагаа гартаа авах хүртэл бүгдийг KublAi хянана.
*(EN: The hardest part: the order. The agent creates it and sends a QPay invoice. The moment payment arrives, the order is confirmed and delivery is on its way. KublAi tracks everything until the product is in the customer's hands.)*

**Sound:** the piano lifts into a fuller arrangement, a satisfying "payment received" tone, a map ping on delivery.

**Motion:** a progress rail along the bottom of the frame lights up step by step: `Зурвас → Гэрээ → Төлбөр → Хүргэлт → Хүргэгдсэн` (Message → Deal → Payment → Delivery → Delivered). This rail is the visual version of the tagline.

**Assets:** recordings of order creation, a QPay test invoice, the order status changes in KublAi ERP, and Kublai Delivery app tracking.

## Style

- **Palette:** dark teal `#041f27`, panel `#06414f`, accent `#5fd4e8`, soft `#d9f6fc`, matching `KublaiGuide`.
- **Type:** Noto Sans (Cyrillic) for everything. Bold for headlines, regular for subtitles.
- **Subtitles:** burned in, bottom center, a 2-line maximum, kept clear of the progress rail.
- **Tone:** calm and confident. The product is the visual, with few effects. Show the agent working, not just the result.
