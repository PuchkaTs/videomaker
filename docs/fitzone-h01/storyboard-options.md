## Revision 2026-11-10 (user's notes): mechanism and research instead of "untrained people"

The user rejected G1 ("дасаагүй хүн гэнэт эхэлбэл…") as unsuitable and asked for how movement lowers stress, backed by research, and for something interesting. Approved («зөвшөөрлөө») and built:

- **G1 (new):** Huberman, "Tools for Managing Stress & Anxiety" (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ntfcfJ28eiU, 59:48–60:37). Stress narrows vision (tunnel vision); deliberately widening the gaze calms a brainstem alertness circuit. Clip `c04`. Picture: his tunnel and wide-arm gestures, a tired worker with the edges closing in, then a wide-horizon walk opening out. This is his explanation, attributed to him, not a settled finding.
- **N5 (new):** Bratman et al. 2015, PNAS (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26124129): 38 adults; a 90-minute nature walk lowered self-reported rumination and subgenual prefrontal cortex blood flow; a walk along a busy road did not. No cortisol was measured, so the card claims none. «нэг л бодлоо давтан эргэцүүлэх» = rumination (needs a native check).
- **Removed:** the sprint shot (`b1`). The old G1 take is kept as `public/fitzone-h01/vo/G1_old.mp3`.
