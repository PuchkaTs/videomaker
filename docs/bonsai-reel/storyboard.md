## Through-line

Nature is far away, out in the mountains, and the reel brings it inside. Ink-wash Mongolian mountains (from the logo and P1's painted wall) → the camera flies through a round moon window → a living bonsai stands in a warm room → the same tree settles into a living room, a desk, a dining table and a hotel lobby → it travels across Mongolia → the viewer picks a tree and comments «1» → the round light closes into the logo's gold brush ring. The round light is the client's own motif (P1, P2, the logo), so it carries the hand-offs.

## Scenes

Each scene lists the options considered and the one chosen (★).

### S1 Hook, 0–3.2 s, V1 «Гэрт тань амьд урлаг дутаж байна уу?»
1. Dark empty stand, a spotlight finds nothing.
2. ★ Ink-wash mountains in 5 depth layers with drifting mist; the camera flies forward and down through them. The title sits in depth between the layers, the mountains pass in front of it and behind it.
3. A grey city flat window, the bonsai's reflection appears.
4. One needle falls in slow motion onto the type.

Chosen: 2. It states the problem (nature is far away) and starts the depth world at once.
- **Text:** «ГЭРТ ТАНЬ / АМЬД УРЛАГ / ДУТАЖ БАЙНА УУ?», warm cream with a gold accent on «АМЬД УРЛАГ». Lines rise out of the mist one by one with a slight z-offset, letter tracking tightening.
- **Camera:** forward dolly with a slow downward tilt, about 6° roll settling to 0, mist layers passing the lens with motion blur.
- **Exit:** a pale round moon (the P1 circle) grows in the centre; the camera flies through it.
- **Sound:** low wind, a single low zither-like pluck under the title; the rising air as the moon grows.

### S2, 3.2–6.4 s, V2 «Бонсай мод бол амьд урлаг.»
1. ★ Through the moon window into P1: Wan clip W1 (slow push-in, needles stirring, light breathing), framed as a plane the camera arrives at, then it fills the frame.
2. Assemble the tree from brush strokes.
3. A split screen of art and tree.

Chosen: 1.

### S4 Places, 10.2–16.8 s, V4 «Гэр, ажлын өрөө, хоолны газар, зочид буудал. Хаана ч тавьсан тансаг харагдана.»

3. A tree that "teleports" between rooms in a match cut on the pot.

## Risks

- The trees drift in shape from the source: every generated frame is compared with P1/P2; failed ones are not used.
- Text over busy foliage: text sits on the darkest layer or its own plane with a soft shadow.
- The CSS-3D sorting pitfalls from KublaiAgentsV2 apply (no opacity on 3D parents; far planes hidden until needed).

## Sound

Music synthesised offline: pentatonic zither-like plucks, soft pad, low drum on the phrase turns, resolving at S7; SFX (wind, chime, wood knocks, a comment "send" tick, bell) on the scene events; ducked under the voice; final -14 LUFS, limiter -1.4 dBFS.
